# Ece Degirmen | Project Portfolio

[GitHub profile](https://github.com/ecedegirmen) · [Repository directory](docs/repository-directory.md) · [Research workflow](docs/research-workflow.md)

I'm a senior at UNC Chapel Hill double majoring in Economics (B.S.) and Data Science (B.A.), with a minor in Statistics & Analytics. My interests span applied economics, public policy, finance, investment management, and business strategy, particularly questions about how people and organizations respond to incentives, uncertainty, and changing conditions.

This portfolio brings together my research, applied analytics and modeling, and finance and investment projects. Each project page explains the problem or question, current stage, my contribution, and next steps. Code, figures, presentations, and written outputs are linked as they are added.

## How I approach the work

I want readers to be able to follow how a question or practical problem becomes an analysis: where the evidence came from, what changed during cleaning or modeling, which assumptions matter, and what the results can truly support. The [research workflow](docs/research-workflow.md) describes the standards I use for research-oriented work, while individual project pages document methods appropriate to each project type.
